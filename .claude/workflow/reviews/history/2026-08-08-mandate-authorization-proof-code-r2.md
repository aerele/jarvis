## Notes on things that held up

- The BLOCKER 1/2/3 repair is the right call. Routing `/return` through `_settle_razorpay_mandate`
  makes the SUBSCRIPTION the money authority, which covers charging and non-charging mandates
  alike, and restores honest pending semantics. S3b and S8b both PASS now, and the live signed
  return answered "Payment confirmed" with `return_url = http://jarvis.local:8002/jarvis/billing?pay=done`.
- MAJOR 6's derivation is sound as a secret: HMAC of `name|generation` under the site key,
  unguessable, changes with the generation, no digest race, and the stranded-customer case is
  gone (every rejected return now carries a real workspace URL — verified live). Its only defect
  is the consumed-stamp interaction (findings 1/9).
- T1 works against reality. Driven end to end on a live test-mode mandate whose confirm was
  deliberately lost, `prove_unrecorded_mandate_money` found the ₹100, named the correct captured
  payment, and moved the money axis — and the re-opened pay page then refused to offer Pay.
- The (purpose, shape) dispatch table and `UnappliableFlow` remain correct and closed.
- The v1_27 patch is idempotent, narrow and reports its rowcount before the commit.
- `_reactivation_mandate_backstop` correctly evaluates `can_reactivate` BEFORE mutating (unlike
  finding 2's site) and refuses a non-lapsed subscription with an ops alert.
