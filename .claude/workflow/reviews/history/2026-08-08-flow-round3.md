## Notes on method

The three seam probes are the core of this round. Reading the diff makes the reactivation look
complete because the browser confirm was taught to apply `intent.target_plan`; executing it shows
that the two seams the system relies on when the browser does **not** come back — the
`payment.captured` webhook and the customer-clickable healer — still activate the old plan. That is
not a corner case: the webhook routinely lands first, and once it does, the browser confirm's own
idempotency guard closes the door.

Re-run this flow review on a lapsed subscription whose tenant site is reachable in an authenticated
browser once findings 1, 2 and 3 are fixed. The four NOT RUN scenarios above (lapsed grid render,
Check-does-not-navigate render, member session, admin unreachable render) must be executed before
any PR.
