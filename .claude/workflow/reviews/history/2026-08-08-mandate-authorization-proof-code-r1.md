## Notes on things that held up

- The (purpose, shape) dispatch table (T2) is genuinely exhaustive and closed; `UnappliableFlow`
  is caught without `mark_applied`, so the apply axis stays honest (`test_the_healer_never_marks_applied_for_a_flow_it_cannot_apply`).
- The double-charge door (T3) works: flow S4 refused a second attempt with
  `PAYMENT_CONFIRMATION_PENDING` and exactly one mandate was ever minted; S5 showed F6 refusing
  the re-open. The mechanism (move the axis, let the existing guards fire) is the right shape.
- The sweep (T7) genuinely restores a customer who never returns: flow S6b →
  `{'checked': 1, 'proved': 1, 'healed': 1}`, subscription Active, `autorenew` 1.
- The earliest-invoice rule, currency check and `["opened_at", "is", "set"]` NULL guard are all
  correct and tested.
- `_blocks_supersede`'s new `apply_state != APPLIED` clause is safe: `_enqueue_release`
  independently refuses to release an object carrying committed money (`intent_ledger.py:798-801`).
